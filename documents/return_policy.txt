BUSINESS RETURN POLICY

Customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.

Return Conditions:
1. Product must be unused.
2. Product must be in original condition.
3. Original packaging must be available.
4. Customer must have the original invoice or order details.
5. Return request must be submitted within 30 days of delivery.

Damaged Products:
Customers must report damaged products within 48 hours of delivery.

Refund:
Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method after the returned product is inspected.

Non-Returnable Items:
1. Used products
2. Products without original packaging
3. Products damaged after customer use
4. Products returned after 30 days

Return Process:
1. Contact customer support.
2. Provide order ID.
3. Explain the reason for return.
4. Wait for return approval.
5. Ship the product according to the provided instructions.
