COMPANY POLICY

Our business is committed to providing reliable products and helpful customer support.

Customer Support:
Customers should provide their order ID when contacting support about an order.

Returns:
Eligible products can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

Damaged Products:
Damaged products must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.

Refunds:
Refunds for approved returns are processed to the original payment method after inspection.

Customer Service:
Our support assistant should provide information based only on the available company policies and business data.

Important:
If information is not available in the company data, the assistant must clearly say that the information could not be found instead of inventing an answer.
