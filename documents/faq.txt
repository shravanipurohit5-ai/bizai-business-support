BUSINESS FAQ

Q: What is the return period?
A: Customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.

Q: Can I return a used product?
A: No. Products must be unused and in original condition.

Q: What should I do if my product arrives damaged?
A: Report the damaged product within 48 hours of delivery.

Q: How will I receive my refund?
A: Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method after inspection.

Q: Do I need the original packaging?
A: Yes. Original packaging is required for eligible returns.

Q: What information is required for a return?
A: Customers should provide their order ID and reason for return.

Q: Can I return a product after 30 days?
A: Normally, returns are accepted only within 30 days of delivery.

Q: How do I contact customer support?
A: Customers can contact the business support team with their order details and question.
